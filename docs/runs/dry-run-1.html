What went wrong, and the fix for each

Each fix goes into code or into the instructions, so the next run doesn't repeat it.

ProblemEvidenceFix
The headless session ended while an agent was still runningThe last message: "The UX review on fc1f4bf is still running"For tests: drive the session until no run is open. Interactive sessions on the desktop or phone wait by themselves.
Too many questions: 12 gates for 2 tasks"LF or CRLF?", "UTF-8 BOM?" are engineering defaults, not product questionsThe chief decides and logs engineering defaults, and asks at most one batched question per task.
Low findings started repair roundsT1's first repair was for a low finding. 11 of 16 findings were lowThe state tool: a repair round needs a medium or high finding. Low ones join a round or get dismissed.
2 cycles ran without autopilotT1 ran a second full cycle, and autopilot was off"Verified" needs 1 clean cycle. Only an autopilot merge needs 2.

Cost

$5.28 for the whole run, at API prices. On your subscription, it counts against your usage limits instead. By number of runs, each task took about half: T1 used 15 runs and T2 14. The design's estimates were too low for small tasks: a small task with a risk flag, 2 repair rounds and 2 cycles cost about $2.60, against an estimate of $0.30 to $1.50. The fixes above (no repair rounds for low findings, 1 cycle unless autopilot) cut that most.
